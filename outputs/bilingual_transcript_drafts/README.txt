BILINGUAL TRANSCRIPT DRAFTS

Four turn-aligned English/regional-language draft transcripts are included, plus a CSV for review. The English column follows the English script supplied in the conversation. Important: do not treat these as completed speech recognition results. The English text is the supplied script, not an independent transcription of the four regional recordings. Reviewers should correct the regional text and speaker turns while listening, especially family history, test values, medicines, diagnoses, and advice. Keep uncertain content marked [unclear].
